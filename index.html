Zhaoyuan Xu

I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Wei Gong. Before that, I received my B.E. degree from USTC in 2020. I will soon join the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong as a postdoctoral researcher, working with Prof. Zhenjiang Li. My work is centered on passive IoT, backscatter communication, and wireless technologies. I am especially interested in how everyday wireless signals can be reused to connect ultra-low-power devices and make sensing systems easier to deploy in the real world.
